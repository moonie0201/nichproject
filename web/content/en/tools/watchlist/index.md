A watchlist is a simple reference tool that keeps the ETFs you care about in one place. With SCHD, JEPI, VYM, JEPQ, QYLD, VOO, and SPY all on screen at once, you can compare dividend yields, expense ratios, historical returns, and risk notes without jumping between pages.

Your list is stored **only in your browser's local storage**. There is no account, no server upload, and no sync across devices or browsers. To share your list with someone else, use the "Share list" button to copy a URL that encodes your current selection.
