## Three Common Errors Dividend Beginners Make

**Error one: yield becomes outcome.** SCHD's 3.09% yield attracts attention, yet reinvested dividend compounding and capital appreciation dominate total return. On a $100,000 position held five years, yield alone contributes roughly $16,000 in dividends; capital appreciation of +60% delivers $60,000 in principal growth. Yield is a component, not the determinant.

**Error two: recent performance substitutes for cycle analysis.** 2025–2026 favored dividend strategies due to rate cuts. The 2021–2022 period showed the inverse — growth equities (VIG holdings) vastly outperformed rate-hike environments. Measurement window selection reverses conclusions. Three-year minimum lookbacks provide more defensible signals than single-year snapshots.

**Error three: dividend reinvestment is optional, not structural.** Leaving distributions in cash yields 3.09% annual returns on SCHD (approximately $3,090 annual on $100,000). Immediate reinvestment triggers compounding that dramatically amplifies results. Tax-deferred accounts (401k, Traditional IRA, Roth IRA) treat reinvested distributions identically to new contributions — no drag — making systematic reinvestment the path to maximum after-tax compounding.
