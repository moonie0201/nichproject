## The Dividend Stability Illusion

High dividend yields frequently seduce investors into underestimating volatility. Dividends represent cash flows, not volatility dampening. JEPI's recent descent to 52-week lows reflects the underlying mechanics: covered call strategies extract maximum premium in high-rate environments. When rates decline (as occurred through 2024–2025), implied volatility contracts sharply, option premiums evaporate, and dividend sustainability erodes. The weakness observed in 2025 directly corresponds to the Fed's rate-cutting cycle—not a temporary dip but a structural headwind for option-selling strategies.
